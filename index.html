How it works

Four steps, repeated every week.

Nothing here is complicated. It is simply done consistently, which is the part most students never get.

01

Diagnose

A short check before your first class tells us exactly which topics are shaky. We start where the gap actually is, not at chapter one.

02

Build

Live on a shared whiteboard, the idea is built from the ground up and the formula is derived in front of you, step by visible step.

03

Drill

Real past questions, timed the way the exam times them. You solve, we mark together, and every wrong step gets traced back to its cause.

04

Track

A weekly note on what improved and what is still weak, sent to you and, if you are in secondary school, to your parent.

Questions

Everything parents and students ask

Which exams do you prepare students for?

SSCE, WAEC, GCE, NECO, JAMB and UTME, and Post-UTME screening. We also teach 100 level university courses in Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology and English.

Are classes live or pre-recorded?

Every class is live on a shared digital whiteboard. You can interrupt, ask, and have the same step worked again until it lands. The session is recorded afterwards so you can revise it before the next class.

How many students are in a class?

Five at most. That limit exists so the tutor can see every student's working and correct mistakes the same day they happen. One-on-one slots are available if you would rather learn alone.

What do I need to join a class?

A phone or laptop, a workable data connection, and a notebook with a pen. The meeting app is free and we send the link before every class. Nothing else to buy.

How much is it and how do I pay?

Core subjects (Mathematics, Further Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, English) are ₦15,000 per month. Programming courses (Python, Web Development) are ₦25,000 per month. You can pay weekly or monthly. Payment details are shared on WhatsApp once your schedule is agreed.

What if a class clashes with school or I miss one?

Tell us before the class and we move it where the schedule allows. If you still miss it, you get the recording and the worksheet, and we cover the questions you got stuck on at the start of the next session.

When do classes start?

Classes are running now. New students can join at any time. Message 0903 914 7388 on WhatsApp and we will confirm your day and time.
