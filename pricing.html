Questions

Everything parents ask before they enrol

How many classes do students get every week?

Every course includes 2 live classes per week. This is the standard flow we use so students stay consistent, revise properly, and make measurable progress before exams.

What is the difference between the BECE and Science packages?

The BECE package is built for JSS3 students preparing for the Basic Education Certificate Examination. The Science package is designed for students focusing on Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry and Biology for WAEC/NECO-style preparation.

What if my child needs one subject only?

That is what the custom plan is for. Each academic course costs ₦8,000, so you only pay for the exact subject your child needs. Programming courses are charged at ₦25,000 each.

Does the fee include revision and homework support?

Yes. Students receive structured lesson notes, revision tasks, question practices, and WhatsApp support for questions between classes.
